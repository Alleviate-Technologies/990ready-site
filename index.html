What 990 Ready does

The numbers are rarely the hard part of a Form 990. The blank boxes are: what your programs achieved, the explanations the form asks for, and the governance questions your board has never discussed.

  1. 1

    Your form and your date

    Enter your EIN. 990 Ready tells you which form you file (990-N, 990-EZ, 990 or 990-PF), when it is due, and what your preparer will ask for.

  2. 2

    Answer in plain English

    Jay, its assistant, asks about your mission, your programs and what they achieved, your board, and your money in round numbers. No line numbers.

  3. 3

    Get the hard parts written

    Program accomplishments and the explanations the form asks for, drafted from your answers the way the IRS expects them, and yours to edit.

  4. 4

    One pack for your preparer

    Every answer mapped to its line, the narratives, and the governance policies the form asks about, ready for your board to adopt.

Three missed years end the exemption. A nonprofit that does not file for three years in a row loses its tax exempt status automatically. 990 Ready keeps the record: each return, its acceptance and its date, year after year.
